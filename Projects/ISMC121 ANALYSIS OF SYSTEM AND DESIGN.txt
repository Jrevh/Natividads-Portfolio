1 PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The Comic Shop Frialde Digital Transformation Initiative aims to provide a fully
integrated web-based application for Your Comic Shop Frialde. The project expects to deliver an
e-commerce platform for both staff and customers of Your Comic Shop Frialde. Customers will
be able to purchase comic books in a way that they can monitor and track their transactions
more accurately and experience transparency through the web application. Staff will be able to
efficiently manage inventory and customer transactions.
2 BACKGROUND OF THE PROJECT
This project addresses the inventory management of the current system. Experiencing
this tedious process during his part-time work for the business, Jearevh has always thought of
ways on how to address this problem. Deleting bought comics from facebook albums with 1000
to 2000 images of comics averagely takes 1 to 2 hours. A sale each month averagely aims to
open 12 to 18 facebook albums in a span of 3 to 8 days with a billing period 3 to 5 days after the
sale period. This puts staff on the clock, prioritizing the billing process before starting on the
deleting process amidst having similar execution of tasks. In addition, facebook can also hinder
access because of repetitive actions such as scrolling through multiple albums successively or
jumping from one image to another quickly.
Problems:
1. Tedious processes
a. Manual billing process
b. Inventory process
c. Removes unnecessary re-upload process due to bogus buyers
d. Problems in Facebook management
2. Schedule constraints
a. Each sale period is scheduled every month where albums should be ready for display.
b. After the sale period, billing also starts every month which necessitates staff to solely
prioritize the billing process.
3 SIGNIFICANCE OF THE PROJECT
The project will smoothen transactions, improve inventory management, and increase
customer interaction. The idea is to address present inefficiencies and deliver a more seamless
purchasing procedure and efficient inventory management. The proposed system will require
less staff for inventory management and billing as the web application innately does these
processes. This will also remove the monthly sale constraint as the processes will happen
instantaneously, removing the requirements of a deleting and billing period in the business
schedule. The system also introduces transparency for customers for each transaction they do
with the business. Overall, this web-application reduces the number of manual processes and
an opportunity for numerous digital improvements for the business.
4 CURRENT SYSTEM
As explained in the background section, the main processes that this project addresses
is the deletion and billing processes. Deleting process spans for around 4 to 10 days depending
on the amount of albums required to process and the number of comics that are bought in each
album. An album with less sales is processed faster because there are few images to delete.
Albums with a large number of sales require much more time because each image has to be
deleted one by one. An image with multiple copies of a comic will have a comment displaying
how many comic stock of that specific comic are left, and the other comments that indicated
previous purchase are deleted. This ensures that for the next deletion process, staff can easily
determine how many copies are left and in the billing process, which are the new purchases.
Billing process is much faster, noting how many copies a customer buys from an album with a
corresponding album price.

5 PROPOSED SYSTEM
The proposed system is a web-based application designed to automate and streamline
business processes. It will include modules for automated transactions, real-time inventory
management, and comprehensive sales analytics. The system will also maintain a robust
customer database and enhance website functionality.

Transaction Processes

multiple items in one transaction.
● In order to track my purchase history as a customer, I can view my past orders.
Sales Analytics
● As a manager, I can view sales reports, so that I can analyze sales trends and make
informed decisions.
● As a sales analyst, when reviewing monthly performance, I want to access detailed sales

5.7 Activity Diagram
This activity diagram shows the perspective of the customer in using the system. The customer,
after logging-in, is brought to the Home page. After browsing for items, the customer can either
end their session or proceed to check-out. The customer has to have completed their account
details, specifically the address for delivery. The order is received by the system and a
confirmation is sent for verification. The staff then checks for the purchased item's availability, if
not a backorder is created. The staff then packages the comic books with details from the
customer purchase receipt. Then a standard shipping procedure is observed, shipping details
reflected in the website through status order. After delivery, the customer may leave a review,
when it is a feedback, staff may respond to let the customer know they are heard.
